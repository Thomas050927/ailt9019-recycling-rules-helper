      Safety first

      Do not mix unknown chemicals or place hazardous materials in ordinary bins. For a laboratory emergency at HKU, the official Safety Office website lists 3917 2882 (24/7 help desk). For chemical-waste procedures, consult the HKU Safety Office Chemicals & Waste page or contact the Chemical Safety Manager at chemical@hku.hk. If there is a spill, exposure, fire, leaking battery, or immediate danger, move away if safe and follow official emergency instructions. This tool is not an emergency service.
